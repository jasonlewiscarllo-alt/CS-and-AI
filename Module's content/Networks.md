#### ***Network definition***
##### **A network is like a group of friends who can communicate with one another, share ideas, and exchange things whenever they need to. In the same way, a computer network is a group of two or more devices, such as computers, mobile phones, and printers, that are connected to each other so they can share information and resources easily. These devices can communicate and exchange data through cables, wires, or wireless signals such as Wi-Fi. For example, when we send a message to a friend, share photos, print a document from a laptop, or use the internet on our phones, we are using a network. Networks make it easier and faster for people to communicate, work together, and share important information. They also help save time and resources because several devices can use the same internet connection, printer, or files. Networks can be small, such as a network connecting devices in a home or school, or very large, connecting computers across different cities and countries. In simple words, a computer network allows different devices to connect, communicate, and share information and resources with one another**


#### **Packets**
![alt text](image-1.png)
##### *In networking, a packet is a small part of a larger piece of information that is sent over the internet. Each packet contains the actual data and a header, which includes important details about the data, such as where it needs to go. The header helps the receiving device understand how to handle the packet.When we send information over the internet, such as a photo, video, or message, the data is divided into smaller packets. These packets travel through network devices like routers and switches until they reach their destination. Once they arrive, the receiving device puts the packets back together so the information can be viewed or used. This process is similar to building a puzzle, where each piece is sent separately and then joined together to complete the picture.Sending data in packets also makes the internet more efficient because many devices can send and receive information at the same time. This method is called packet switching. Instead of one connection taking up the whole network, packets can travel through available routes, allowing many people to use the internet simultaneously.*
