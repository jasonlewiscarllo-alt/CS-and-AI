Study, Review, and Coordination Protocol
Investigation & Drafting: Members research assigned topics independently, draft notes in local Markdown branches, and open pull requests for team visibility.

Peer Review & Correction: Before any topic is marked [Checked], at least one other member reviews the technical accuracy, clarity for beginners, and asset link integrity. Corrections are committed directly to the shared repository.

Communication & Meetings: Weekly synchronous alignment meetings in real life if there is available time, supplemented by asynchronous task tracking on GitHub Projects.

Rehearsals & Testing: Practical activities and mini-lecture walkthroughs will be rehearsed among team members at least 48 hours prior to live delivery or submission deadlines.

Risk Management & Mitigation Strategy
Risk 1 (Difficult Content): Concepts like Hardware may prove overly complex for beginners.

Mitigation: Break topics down into micro-subtopics, incorporate extra visual diagrams, and schedule an extra peer review pass.

Risk 2 (Uneven Workloads or Bottlenecks): Member illness or scheduling conflicts.

Mitigation: If one member is sick another member will take over the work to avoid lagging behind and if everyone is sick then just push through.
